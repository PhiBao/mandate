How it works

1

Claim a wallet

Run /claim <wallet>, sign the exact EIP-191 message with your private key. The bot verifies signer == wallet and timestamps the claim — permanently. Claiming 5 wallets to promote the winner is impossible: all claims bind and all count.

2

Forward-only tracking

Closed positions are ingested from your claim timestamp only. Anything before the claim doesn't exist. Retroactive cherry-picking is structurally impossible, not just discouraged.

3

Honest verdicts

Mean return is bootstrapped into a 95% confidence interval and benchmarked against BTC. If the interval includes zero, the card says so: not distinguishable from luck — the line incumbents won't ship.

4

Standing on every call

Anyone posting a call gets a standing card attached — including UNVERIFIED for members who never claimed. Refusal to verify is a first-class signal, visible to the whole room.

Commands

/claim <wallet> [signature]

Two-step wallet claim: get the message, sign it, send back the signature.

/standing [@user]

Full standing card — tier, trades, win rate, CI, drawdown, wallets claimed.

/why [@user]

The derivation trail: claim hash, evidence, exclusions, CI method, tier reasons.

/search <query>

Search traders and verdicts across persistent memory.
